# Clipboard.im

### Your next device is one link away.

Clipboard.im is a lightweight online clipboard for moving text, links, and files between devices. Paste on your computer, open on your phone, or share with someone else — all in a browser, without signing up or installing an app.

## Made for the small transfers that interrupt your day

- **Start right away.** No account, login, app, or browser extension required.
- **One link, multiple devices.** Open the same clipboard address on a phone, tablet, or computer. Customize the address to make it easier to remember.
- **Text and files together.** Paste notes or links, drag in files, then copy or download them on another device.
- **Scan and continue.** Use the built-in QR code to open your clipboard on a phone without typing the address.
- **Choose how to share.** Add a clipboard password, or create a read-only link so recipients can view text and download files without editing or deleting them. Read-only links can have a separate password and can be revoked.
- **Fits your setup.** Responsive layouts, light and dark themes, and an interface available in 22 languages.

## When it comes in handy

Move a link from your phone to your laptop. Bring a document to a shared computer for printing. Pass meeting notes and files to a colleague through a read-only link.

## Get started in three steps

1. Open the website to get a clipboard address.
2. Paste text or upload files. Click outside the text field to save your changes.
3. Open the same address on another device, or scan the QR code. Copy the text or download the files.

## Current limits and access

The website currently lists **100 MB per file**, **300 MB per clipboard**, and automatic file cleanup after **7 days**. Check the upload area for current limits. Keep a separate copy of important files; this is a temporary handoff tool.

Without a clipboard password, anyone who knows its address can access and change its contents. Use a read-only link when others only need to view or download. A password controls access; keep it private.
